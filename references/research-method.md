# Research method

Use the fixed flow: entity identification → Plan → Research → Verify → Synthesize. Default to standard research; quick research still returns all 43 summary rows, with transparent evidence gaps.

## Entity identification

Confirm the standard/legal name, official website, headquarters, listed or registered entity, and scope. Do not mix parent, subsidiary, brand, or namesake information. Ask the user to clarify unresolved ambiguity.

## Plan

Use the 43 summary fields and the repeat-record structures in [company-profile-fields.md](company-profile-fields.md). Set a priority source, reporting period, currency, geography, and consolidation scope for each high-impact question before research.

Prioritize: identity/governance/capital; products/business/customers/value chain; three-year finance and operating signals; industry/policy/cycle/competition; technology and market position; compliance/litigation/penalties; risks and conditional outlook.

## Research and verification

Search in this order: company and IR materials; original filings and exchanges; regulators, governments, courts and official databases; official policy, sanctions, and export-control sources; original industry research and reputable media as needed.

For every usable evidence item retain title, URL, publication date, access date, applicable information cutoff, scope, and caveat. Use direct primary sources for financials, funding/listing, ownership, penalties, litigation, sanctions/export controls, major customers, market share, and material technical claims.

Perform a minimum compliance, litigation, and penalty search in every standard research. A non-searchable or inaccessible database is a coverage limitation, never a negative finding.

When sources conflict, compare entity, publication date, reporting period, definition, geography, currency, and consolidation scope. Present unresolved conflicts as needing verification.

## Synthesis

- Markdown carries detailed evidence, full URLs, conflict treatment, repeat-record tables, calculation method, and reasoning.
- CSV carries the 43-row structured-data attachment; keep text compact and use up to two key URLs.
- The five-sheet workbook carries decision cards, product/customer/competitor matrices, timeline, evidence-linked ratings and IT opportunities, and the full source index.

Every fact gets a fact ID. A judgment or opportunity must link to the underlying fact/evidence ID. Never use an unverified claim to fill a missing field.

For compatibility, do not set frozen panes on any workbook sheet. After export, check the workbook package rather than relying on a screenshot: no worksheet pane may have `state="frozen"`.
